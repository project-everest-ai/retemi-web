Retemi Pilot Privacy Notice

Draft · Pre-pilot · For counsel review · Not legally approved or effective. The effective date remains a publication gate. Qualified counsel must review this notice against the released product, vendors, pilot scope, and applicable law before participant enrollment.

Effective: [PILOT START DATE] · Operator: Project Everest LLC · Product: Retemi

Retemi is a limited, pre-release pilot offered by Project Everest LLC. The pilot is designed to help us evaluate Retemi’s experience, guidance, functionality, privacy protections, and usefulness before a broader release.

You remain in control of information about you.

About this pilot

Retemi helps you explore healthspan through areas such as movement, sleep, nutrition, Community, and guidance from Ask Retemi.

This pilot is for evaluation and feedback. It is not medical care, does not create a doctor-patient relationship, and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency services.

The pilot is intended only for adults age 18 or older and residents of Texas.

Please do not enter identifying or health information about another person—including an athlete, client, patient, family member, or person you care for—unless Retemi specifically provides an authorized sharing process for that information.

During the pilot, use fictional or non-identifying information when testing scenarios involving other people.

Information Retemi may process

Depending on how you use the pilot, Retemi may process information you provide when:

  • creating or using your account;
  • selecting roles, interests, or areas of focus;
  • building or updating your Retemi Foundation;
  • asking questions or providing information to Ask Retemi;
  • using Community features;
  • creating connections, activities, or interactions;
  • providing pilot feedback; and
  • otherwise interacting with Retemi.

Retemi may also process technical information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and evaluate the application.

Some information you voluntarily provide may reveal information about your:

  • health;
  • wellbeing;
  • physical activity;
  • sleep;
  • nutrition;
  • physical capabilities;
  • goals;
  • recovery;
  • lifestyle; or
  • personal circumstances.

Project Everest LLC treats this type of information as sensitive.

Retemi will not assume that Community participation, a connection to another person, a coaching or caregiving role, or an organizational relationship gives anyone permission to view your private healthspan information.

Why we use your information

Project Everest LLC uses pilot information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • provide and personalize the Retemi pilot;
  • respond to your requests;
  • operate Ask Retemi and other features you choose to use;
  • maintain security and reliability;
  • troubleshoot problems;
  • understand how pilot features are used;
  • obtain and evaluate pilot feedback; and
  • improve Retemi before broader release.

We do not sell your personal information or health-related information.

We do not use your health-related information for targeted advertising.

We do not give another Retemi user, coach, caregiver, organization, Community participant, or other person access to your private healthspan information merely because you are connected to them.

Any sharing with another person through Retemi should result from a feature or action that clearly indicates what you are choosing to share.

Service providers

Project Everest LLC may use contracted technology providers to operate infrastructure, authentication, communications, security, analytics, artificial-intelligence functionality, or other services necessary to provide and evaluate the pilot.

Those providers may process information only as reasonably necessary to perform services for Project Everest LLC and subject to the privacy, security, and contractual restrictions applicable to those services.

Project Everest LLC does not authorize service providers to sell Retemi health-related information or use it for targeted advertising.

Ask Retemi and artificial intelligence

Ask Retemi may use artificial-intelligence technology to respond to questions and provide guidance.

Information you provide to Ask Retemi may be processed by technology providers used by Project Everest LLC to operate that functionality.

You should provide only information that is reasonably necessary for the guidance you are seeking.

Do not enter another person’s identifiable health or medical information unless Retemi expressly provides an authorized method for doing so.

Ask Retemi may provide incomplete, inaccurate, or inappropriate information. Retemi guidance should be considered informational and supportive rather than a substitute for professional medical judgment.

Health applications and connected devices

Unless Retemi expressly tells you otherwise and asks for your permission, the pilot does not obtain health information from external health applications, wearable devices, medical providers, or health records.

If Retemi later offers a connection to Apple Health, Garmin, another wearable, a healthcare provider, or another health-information source, that connection will be subject to additional privacy review and any permission or consent appropriate to that feature.

Choosing not to connect an external source will not prevent you from using unrelated Retemi features.

Community and your healthspan information

Community is an important part of Retemi’s healthspan model.

Using Community, connecting with another Person, participating in an activity, working with a coach, caring for someone, or having a relationship with an organization does not by itself authorize the sharing of your private healthspan information.

Your relationships within Retemi and your private healthspan information should remain separate unless you intentionally choose to share specific information through a feature designed for that purpose.

Pilot account and retention boundaries

Your Retemi pilot account is temporary. Project Everest LLC does not currently plan to transfer pilot accounts or identifiable pilot healthspan information into a production version of Retemi.

Retention periods and treatment of backups, shared records, security evidence, and legally retained records remain subject to operational validation and counsel review. This draft does not promise a deletion timeline.

See Account & data for the current distinction between leaving the pilot and requesting account/data deletion.

You may leave the pilot at any time

Participation in the Retemi pilot is voluntary. You may stop using Retemi at any time.

You may also request, as applicable:

  • access to personal information associated with your pilot account;
  • correction of inaccurate personal information;
  • a copy of your personal information; or
  • deletion of your personal information and pilot account.

You may withdraw consent to the processing of sensitive information.

Withdrawal will not affect processing that lawfully occurred before your withdrawal, but Project Everest LLC will stop processing that information for purposes requiring your consent except where limited retention is legally permitted or required.

Privacy and deletion requests may be submitted through the Account & data page using the listed external request channel. Do not include health details in an email request.

Project Everest LLC may take reasonable steps to verify that a request relates to the person making the request before acting on it.

Security

Project Everest LLC uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect pilot information.

No application, computer system, transmission method, network, or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

If Project Everest LLC discovers a security incident involving your information that requires notification under applicable law, Project Everest LLC will provide any legally required notice.

Health information and HIPAA

Retemi is currently being developed as a consumer healthspan guidance application and is not intended to function as your healthcare provider, health plan, or medical record.

Information provided directly to a consumer application is not necessarily protected by the federal Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act, commonly known as HIPAA.

Different legal obligations may apply if Retemi later receives protected health information from, or provides services on behalf of, a healthcare provider, health plan, or other regulated entity.

Regardless of whether a particular law classifies information as protected health information, Project Everest LLC treats identifiable health-related information provided to Retemi as sensitive information and applies the protections described in this Pilot Privacy Notice.

Children

The Retemi pilot is intended only for adults age 18 or older.

Do not create a pilot account for a child or provide identifiable health information about a child during the pilot.

Changes during the pilot

Retemi is a pre-release product, and features may change during the pilot.

If Project Everest LLC proposes a change that materially affects how sensitive information is collected, used, disclosed, or shared, we will provide additional notice and obtain additional consent where required before applying that change to your information.

Separate health and sensitive information consent

This is planned separate consent language. Reading or acknowledging this Privacy Notice does not provide this consent. The released app must present this consent separately and must not preselect it.

Retemi may process health-related and other sensitive information that I choose to provide during this pilot.

I consent to Project Everest LLC processing the health-related and other sensitive information I choose to provide for the purposes described in the Retemi Pilot Privacy Notice.

I understand that participation is voluntary, that I can withdraw my consent and request deletion, and that my pilot account and identifiable pilot information are not intended to carry over into the production release of Retemi.

A Person who does not provide this separate consent should not be permitted to enter health-related or other sensitive information into pilot features that require such processing.
