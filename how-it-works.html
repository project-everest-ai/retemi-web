How Retemi works

Guidance shaped by your context.

Retemi is a general-wellness product that helps people explore practical next steps across sleep, nutrition, exercise, and everyday healthspan goals.

Foundation

Add broad context when useful. Foundation setup is optional after pilot admission.

Ask Retemi

Ask a question and receive bounded guidance informed by the eligible context you choose to use.

Quick Workout

Explore structured exercise ideas with duration, effort, equipment, and safety constraints.

Insights

Reflect on context and progress without treating an insight as a diagnosis or promised outcome.

Connected experiences

The current product architecture also includes Me, Community, Organizations/workspaces, and supported Coach and Caregiver perspectives. Availability may vary during the pre-release pilot; experimental or incomplete capabilities are not promised.

Safety and professional care

Eligible AI-supported guidance runs only after deterministic safety checks. Retemi can disable AI independently and retain a deterministic fallback. Guidance may be wrong or incomplete. Retemi does not diagnose, treat, provide emergency care, or replace a qualified professional.

Emergency? Call 911 or your local emergency service. For urgent or individualized health concerns, contact a qualified healthcare professional.
