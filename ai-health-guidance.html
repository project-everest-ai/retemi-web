AI & health guidance

Bounded support, not medical care.

Some eligible Retemi guidance may use AI. Retemi limits this use to supported wellness domains and keeps safety decisions outside provider generation.

How the boundary works

  • Deterministic safety checks run before eligible provider generation.
  • AI capabilities are domain-bounded and can be independently disabled.
  • A deterministic fallback remains available when AI is off or unavailable.
  • Operational evidence about AI use is metadata-only, not a second health record.

Limits

AI-generated guidance may be inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate. Retemi does not provide diagnosis, treatment, comprehensive medical triage, emergency monitoring, or guaranteed safety or accuracy. No clinician reviews every response.

Get the right help. Call 911 or local emergency services for an emergency. Contact a qualified professional for symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, medication decisions, or individualized medical advice.
