Limited Texas pilot

Participation by invitation.

Retemi is preparing a controlled, pre-release pilot for named participants.

  • Participants must be age 18 or older.
  • Participants must be Texas residents.
  • Admission is invite-only and subject to the server-authoritative cohort cap.
  • Creating or authenticating an account does not grant pilot access.
  • Foundation setup is optional after admission.
  • Participation is voluntary, and pre-release features may change or be withdrawn.
No open enrollment. This site does not create an account or admit anyone to the pilot. You may request information; admission requires a separate named invitation and eligibility process.

Before participation

Final participant terms, privacy notice, consent language, operational readiness, and counsel review remain launch gates.
