Pilot terms

A clear boundary for learning together.

Draft · Pre-pilot · For counsel review · Not legally approved or effective. Qualified counsel must tailor these terms to the released product, entity, participants, and jurisdiction before use. This draft is not legal advice and does not create an agreement.

Draft version: October 11, 2026

Private pilot

Retemi is an evolving pre-release product. Features may be incomplete, change, or be withdrawn. Pilot access should be limited to invited, eligible adults who reside in Texas and accept the final terms and acknowledge the final Privacy Notice.

General wellness only

Retemi provides general educational and wellness support. It does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment, emergency monitoring, or professional medical, nutrition, legal, or other regulated advice. Participants should use their judgment, stop an activity that feels unsafe, and seek qualified care when appropriate. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

Community and activities

Project Everest does not organize, inspect, supervise, certify, or endorse participant-created activities merely by making them visible. Hosts and participants are responsible for suitability, location, equipment, supervision, permissions, and safety. The final terms must address reporting, moderation, removal, prohibited conduct, and the treatment of coach or professional claims.

Participant responsibilities

  • Provide accurate account information and protect account access.
  • Share only information they are authorized to share.
  • Respect consent, privacy, intellectual property, and the safety of others.
  • Do not misuse Retemi, attempt unauthorized access, or rely on it for emergencies.
  • Report material security or safety concerns promptly.

Feedback and confidentiality

The pilot is intended to generate candid feedback. Before enrollment, Project Everest should decide whether confidentiality or an NDA is appropriate, how feedback may be used, and whether participant submissions grant a license. These obligations should not be improvised in the application.

Accounts, suspension, and deletion

The final product must explain how participants leave the pilot, close an account, export or delete information, and appeal an access restriction. Project Everest may need to suspend access to protect people or the service, consistent with the final terms and applicable law.

Legal provisions still required

Counsel should complete warranties and disclaimers, limitation of liability, indemnity if appropriate, governing law and venue, dispute resolution, electronic communications, assignment, severability, changes, and contact details. No placeholder here should be treated as an enforceable provision.

Contact

Questions about the planned pilot may be submitted through the Retemi contact page or sent to ceo@projecteverest.company.
