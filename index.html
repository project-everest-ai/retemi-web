Healthspan first

Improved healthspan is our north star.

Healthspan is the amount of life spent living with strength, independence, resilience, purpose, and wellbeing—not simply the number of years lived.

Retemi brings goals, context, action, and reflection together so each next step can become more useful and grounded over time.

A dependable point of reference

Why Retemi?

Retemi takes its inspiration from the rete—the rotating “star-net” at the heart of a planispheric astrolabe, one of history’s great instruments for understanding the sky and finding direction.

The rete carried pointers for prominent stars and rotated over the instrument’s underlying coordinate plate. Astrolabes helped people make astronomical calculations, tell time, find direction, and navigate using the sky.

Retemi carries that idea forward. Sleep, nutrition, movement, caregiving, and coaching all change with your circumstances. Rather than prescribe one fixed path, Retemi helps you understand your context, find a practical next step, and keep moving toward better healthspan.

The North Star is our symbol for that purpose: a steady point of reference—not a promise of a perfect route, but something dependable to navigate toward.

Healthspan is personal

Understanding without blame or promises

Health outcomes are influenced by movement, sleep, nutrition, relationships, healthcare, environment, genetics, illness, injury, opportunity, and life circumstances. Many of these factors are not fully within anyone’s control.

Retemi aims to help people better understand what may be useful, make informed choices, and adapt as life changes—without judgment or a promised outcome.
