Account & data

Leaving and deletion are different requests.

Leave the Retemi pilot

Withdrawal ends your active participation and access under the pilot workflow. It does not by itself mean that every account or data record has been destructively deleted.

Request account/data deletion

The product can record a verified deletion request. The destructive deletion processor and final rules for retention, backups, shared records, security evidence, and legally retained records are not yet finalized.

No deletion timeline is promised. Final processing and retention language remains subject to operational validation and counsel review.

Safest interim request channel

Email privacy@projecteverest.company with the subject Retemi deletion request. Send the request from the email address associated with your Retemi account so the team can begin verification.

Do not include health information, passwords, access codes, or other sensitive details in the email. The team may take reasonable steps to verify that the request relates to you.
